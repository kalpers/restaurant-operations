This application is free for noncommercial use under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Commercial use needs a separate commercial license from the developer, Kneale Alpers.

## Do I need a commercial license?

You need one if you, or the organization you work for, will:

- run the application to operate a restaurant, bar or other business, even internally;
- sell, resell or rebrand the application or a modified version of it;
- offer it to others as a hosted service (SaaS);
- build it, or parts of it, into a commercial product or paid client work.

You do **not** need one for personal projects, study, research, evaluating or testing it, or for use by charities, schools, public research organizations or government institutions. The [LICENSE](LICENSE) has the exact terms.

If you're not sure, ask.

## How to request one

Email **restaurantops.licensing@gmail.com** with:

1. your name, company and country;
2. how you plan to use the application (for example, running your own restaurants, reselling it, or hosting it for customers);
3. how many locations or customers it will serve;
4. whether you plan to modify the code;
5. when you'd like to start.

You'll get a reply with terms. Nothing is licensed until both sides agree in writing.

If you'd rather not email, you can open a [Commercial license inquiry](../../issues/new?template=commercial-license.yml) issue on GitHub. **Issues are public**, so leave out anything confidential and we'll continue by email.
